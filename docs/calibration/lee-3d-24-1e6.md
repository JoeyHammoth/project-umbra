# LEE calibration — 3-D, 10⁶ toys on the 24³ volume

The 3-D criterion could not be decided where it was first measured. The `40³`
scene ([`lee-3d-40-2e4.md`](lee-3d-40-2e4.md)) put the rate at 1.100e-3 with a
Wilson interval running from 7.266e-4 to 1.665e-3 — a point 1.8% inside the band's
floor, so "the whole interval inside ±20%" wanted a half-width of 2e-5 and the
report's `needs` came out at 11 007 432 toys. A toy costs 10.9 ms there, which is
32 hours on sixteen threads: the criterion was open by budget, not by physics.

A smaller cube fixes the budget. Per-toy cost on this bench, measured on 4 000
toys per scene (seed `0x5ca1ab1e`):

| Scene | Rays | ms/toy, 16 threads | Extrapolation ratio at 3σ / 4σ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40³ | 1 600 | 10.9 | 1.02 / 1.04 |
| 32³ | 1 024 | 5.4 | 1.04 / 1.22 |
| 24³ | 576 | 2.2 | 1.08 / 0.97 |

`24³` is five times cheaper per toy and still inside the spec's 20% target at both
ratios, so it is the volume scene: `UMBRA_LEE_GRID` now defaults to it in 3-D, and
10⁶ toys cost 42 minutes rather than a week. The scene is not chosen to make the
rate pass — a smaller cube means a smaller look-elsewhere factor (532× against
the `40³` scene's 724×) and its own threshold (4.81σ against 5.05σ) — it is chosen
so the question can be answered at all. The executable version of that argument is
the report's `needs` field: at `40³` no affordable sample decides, here 202 245
toys are enough and the run is five times that.

Reproduced by `env UMBRA_LEE_DIM=3 UMBRA_LEE_GRID=24 UMBRA_LEE_TOYS=1000000
UMBRA_LEE_SEED=0x600d1de5 UMBRA_LEE_THREADS=16 cargo bench -p umbra-stats --bench
lee`. The seed is fresh: not the `40³` record's, not the pilot's. The report below
is that run's output, verbatim.

## What the run decided, and what it did not

1233 of 1 000 000 toys clear the model's 4.81σ threshold: rate **1.233e-3**,
Wilson 95% [1.166e-3, 1.304e-3], inside the ±20% band [1.080e-3, 1.620e-3] —
**PASS**, on a sample five times what the point estimate needs.

The rate is 9% *below* nominal, with an interval that excludes nominal entirely:
what the criterion asks for is ±20%, and that is what it meets. Saying so matters,
because the plane errs the other way (1.420e-3, 5% above) and a reader who took the
point estimates at face value would conclude the two search spaces disagree about
the sign of the error. Both are consistent with a model good to a few percent at
3σ, which is what the ratios above show.

The law that produces the threshold holds where it is now testable: 1.09×, 1.12×,
1.08×, 1.05× and 1.08× from 3σ to 5σ. The 5σ row is the first in this campaign's
history with toys in it — 480 of them — so for the first time the extrapolation
there is measured rather than quoted alone.

The `24³` scene's `χ̄(u₀) = 6.091 ± 0.003` is smaller than the `40³` scene's
19.599 ± 0.046 in proportion to the ray count, which is the Euler characteristic
following the number of columns rather than the rate: the two scenes' χ values are
not comparable, and only ratios across them are.
